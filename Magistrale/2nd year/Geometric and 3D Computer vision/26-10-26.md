**discrete fourier transform**

having discrete function : $f: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{C}$ integer to complex 

it's periodic after M samples : $f(i)=f(i+M)\forall i$ 

function = array of M samples 

>[!important] 
>There exists M complex coefficents ($F(0), \dots , F(M-1)$) such that 
>$$
>f(x) = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{u=0}^{M-1}F(u)e^{j2\pi xu /M}
>$$ 
>we can reconstruct the original signal with linear combination of complex exponential multiplied by some coefficents that always exists 

fourier bases function -> the complex exponetial -> always the same except for the / M 

$e^{j2\pi xu /M}$ harmonics 

can be expanded as : 
$$
\left( \cos\left( \frac{2\pi u}{M} x\right) + j \sin\left( \frac{2\pi u}{M} x\right) \right)
$$
with euler formula
$x$ value of the function 
$u$ sum counter

u = 4 -> sin and cos 4 periods over the span of M samples 

magnitude > how large oscillation is -> real part
imaginary -> sets the starting point of sin/ cos phase
