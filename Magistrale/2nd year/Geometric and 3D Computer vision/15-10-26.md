convolution -> instead of flipping -> 
$$
(w \star f)(x,y)=\sum \sum w(s,t) f(x-s,y-t)
$$
$*$ -> correlation symbol

conv properties : 
+ $F \star G = G \star F$ comm
+ $(F \star G) \star H = F \star (G \star H)$ ass
+  $(aF+bG) \star H = aF \star H + b G \star H$ lin

a filter is a piece of the image itself -> correlation with the image itself (subtract mean from image and filter) -> output -> white in the spot where filter was present
